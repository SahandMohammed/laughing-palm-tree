# LogicBloom × Privé — Product Launch Film

## 1. Production Summary

**Format:** Vertical social launch film  
**Resolution:** 1080 × 1920  
**Frame rate:** 30 FPS  
**Target duration:** 42–48 seconds  
**Target:** Instagram Reel / Story / portfolio showcase  
**Renderer:** HyperFrames  
**Animation:** GSAP + CSS/SVG + deterministic HyperFrames timeline  
**Theme:** Light  
**Primary style:** Premium editorial + liquid glass + real product interfaces

This is not a generic technology advertisement.

The film should feel like a premium digital-product launch showing how LogicBloom transformed the Privé experience across branding, web, infrastructure, management software, and the upcoming mobile experience.

---

# 2. Creative Principle

## Main idea

> A great experience shouldn't end at the door.

Privé already has a physical experience.

LogicBloom extends that experience digitally.

The film progresses from:

**physical brand**

→ **digital identity**

→ **website**

→ **technology behind the business**

→ **management platform**

→ **mobile customer experience**

→ **LogicBloom × Privé**

The visual narrative should progressively reveal that LogicBloom did not simply make a website.

It built a connected digital ecosystem around Privé.

---

# 4. Material Language

## Liquid glass

Liquid glass is an interaction and transition material.

It is NOT the brand identity.

Use it for:

- foreground lenses
- interface extraction
- panels
- transitions
- floating UI fragments
- light refraction
- depth layers
- device transitions

The actual branding should remain crisp.

### Glass appearance

Prefer:

- white / icy-blue tint
- very subtle navy reflections
- minimal green edge lighting
- transparent highlights
- soft refraction
- strong edge definition
- restrained shadows

Avoid:

- rainbow glass
- excessive chromatic aberration
- colorful Apple-Vision-Pro imitation
- thick glossy plastic
- excessive glow

---

# 5. Typography Direction

Typography should be extremely confident.

Use:

- very large headings
- short phrases
- tight tracking
- editorial spacing
- strong visual hierarchy

Avoid paragraphs appearing as graphics.

Text animation should be used primarily for:

- major narrative beats
- chapter transitions
- key product statements

Examples:

> MORE THAN A WEBSITE.

> BUILT AROUND THE BUSINESS.

> IN THE RIGHT HANDS.

> THE EXPERIENCE CONTINUES.

---

# 6. Audio Direction

The film contains three audio layers.

## A. Voiceover

Voice is the primary timing source.

Style:

- male
- calm
- premium
- intelligent
- warm
- understated
- confident
- approximately 0.90–0.95 speaking speed

Avoid:

- trailer voice
- exaggerated bass voice
- high-energy advertising delivery
- overly emotional delivery

---

## B. Music

### Music brief

Premium minimal electronic product-launch score.

Characteristics:

- ambient opening
- subtle pulse
- modern electronic texture
- restrained low-end
- elegant glass-like percussion
- gradual rhythmic development
- strong middle section
- resolved ending
- no vocals

Avoid:

- generic corporate music
- EDM
- orchestral movie-trailer music
- aggressive drums
- inspirational piano corporate tracks

Suggested generation intent:

> Premium minimal electronic technology launch score, sophisticated ambient opening, elegant digital pulse, subtle cinematic low end, glass-like percussion and textures, gradual build, confident rhythmic middle section, polished resolved ending, no vocals, modern luxury technology aesthetic.

---

## C. Sound Design

Use selectively.

Suggested sounds:

- glass sweep
- subtle impact
- low-frequency transition hit
- UI confirmation
- restrained click
- short reverse swell
- soft digital shimmer

Do not add a sound effect to every animation.

# 9. Timing Philosophy

The generated voiceover determines final duration.

Do NOT artificially compress the voice to reach a predetermined runtime.

If narration becomes:

45 seconds

make a ~48-second film.

If narration becomes:

54 seconds

make a ~57-second film.

Always leave approximately 1.5–2 seconds of visual hold after the final narration.

# 13. Motion Rules

## Allowed

- GSAP
- CSS transforms
- inline SVG
- deterministic displacement
- masks
- clip paths
- 3D transforms
- perspective
- motion blur where render-safe
- camera-like movement
- light sweeps
- restrained grain

## Avoid

- random values
- free-running CSS animations
- timers
- requestAnimationFrame loops
- wall-clock calculations
- scroll-driven runtime animation
- hover effects
- uncontrolled Three.js animation
- unnecessary particles

Everything important must be seekable from the HyperFrames timeline.

---

# 14. Visual Quality Rules

Never:

- invent a Privé logo
- type the brand name instead of using the available logo asset
- invent a fake website if the real site is available
- invent fake screenshots when real product surfaces exist
- change brand colors arbitrarily
- introduce gradients unrelated to the LogicBloom/Privé systems
- overuse glass
- make every element float
- use random glass bubbles just because they look futuristic

Always prioritize:

1. real brand
2. real product
3. typography
4. composition
5. motion
6. glass effects

in that order.
