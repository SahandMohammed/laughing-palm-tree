# Production Design System

- Navy: `#0E2247`
- Green: `#2F6657`
- Muted text: `#526074`
- Separator: `#8B95A5`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Soft background: `#F7F9FC`
- Blue background: `#EAF0F8`
- Pearl: `#FBFDFF`
- Ice: `#ECF5FC`
- Pale blue: `#CDDFF0`
- Steel blue: `#9CB8D5`
- Soft navy: `#5478A3`

## Theme

Light, premium, editorial.

White / icy blue is the environment. Navy carries the visual weight. Green is a restrained accent only.

## Brand hierarchy

Privé is the subject. LogicBloom is the creator framing the story.

Use the **real logo assets**. Never redraw either brand with typed text when the logo itself is being presented.

## Liquid glass

Glass is a supporting material, not the identity:

- white / ice tint
- navy shadow
- minimal green edge accent
- crisp highlights
- restrained refraction
- limited use at transitions and product framing

## Product truth

The real website capture is shown as a real product surface. Missing management/mobile/invitation screenshots are represented abstractly and explicitly, never as fake screenshots.
