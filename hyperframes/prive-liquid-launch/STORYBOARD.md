# Storyboard — Privé Liquid Glass Launch

**Format:** 1080 × 1920  
**Duration:** 12 seconds  
**FPS:** 30  
**Style:** premium white liquid glass / editorial launch film

| Time | Scene | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–1.7s | MORE THAN GROOMING. | Oversized type rises in; liquid lens swells and drifts through foreground. |
| 1.7–3.2s | IN THE RIGHT HANDS. | Three-line statement lands rapidly while a translucent glass mass morphs behind it. |
| 3.2–5.8s | THE PRIVÉ EXPERIENCE. | Glass phone rises in 3D; booking UI resolves; three floating capability pills appear around it. |
| 5.8–7.4s | BOOK. GROOM. RETURN. | Bold kinetic words hit in sequence; wide liquid-glass slice sweeps across with a specular shine. |
| 7.4–9.6s | DIGITAL, DONE RIGHT. | Website, booking and mobile-experience cards cascade in like product modules. |
| 9.6–12.0s | PRIVÉ | Final logo lockup, “in the right hands.” and privelounge.co over a soft glass orb. |

## Motion rules

- Every major entrance is fast and confident: ~0.3–0.7 s.
- Scene exits are shorter than entrances to keep momentum.
- Glass shapes move more slowly than typography to create depth.
- No free-running CSS animations; GSAP controls timeline state.
- Background orbs move continuously across all 12 seconds so cuts still feel connected.
